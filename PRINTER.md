## Printer

The [Original Prusa MK4S](https://www.prusa3d.com/category/original-prusa-mk4s/) is part of Prusa Research's MK line. Its features include a high-flow nozzle and 360° print cooling.

## Slicing software

[PrusaSlicer](https://www.prusa3d.com/en/page/prusaslicer_426/) is an open-source slicer maintained by Prusa Research. Prusa advertises more than 180 tested, automatically updated filament and resin profiles for its printers.

PrusaSlicer can export `.bgcode` files for printing. See Prusa's [binary G-code overview](https://blog.prusa3d.com/prusa-autumn-news-summary-2023-new-firmware-prusaslicer-mk3-5-info-and-more_86935/) for details.

## Filament

PLA is the primary material currently used. For project planning, assume PLA and the installed 0.4 mm high-flow nozzle unless a project specifies otherwise.

The filament brand, product variant, color, and preferred print settings have not yet been recorded.
